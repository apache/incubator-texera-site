<p class="post-eyebrow">May 14&ndash;18, 2026 &middot; UC Irvine</p>

<p class="post-lead">We are thrilled to celebrate the successful conclusion of the recent dkNET-AI &middot; Apache Texera Agent Hackathon! Brought together by the NIH dkNET project and hosted by the Apache Texera team, this intensive, in-person event challenged participants to build the future of human-AI collaborative data science.</p>

<p class="post-lead">Over a dynamic four-day sprint from May 14 to May 18, 2026, at UC Irvine, the event united researchers, students, and data enthusiasts to build intuitive, visual workflows where advanced AI agents co-author data pipelines alongside humans.</p>

<h2 data-num="02">Final Award Winners &amp; Highlights</h2>

<p>Our distinguished panel of judges&mdash;<a href="https://www.lu-xi.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Xi Lu</a> (University at Buffalo), <a href="https://zuozhiw.github.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Zuozhi Wang</a> (Snowflake), and <a href="https://yicong-huang.github.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Yicong Huang</a> (Databricks)&mdash;evaluated the submissions based on craft, real workflow leverage, and human-in-the-loop design. Top-performing projects and runners-up were recognized with individual gift card awards ranging from $50 to $100.</p>
