## Becoming a Committer

Anyone can contribute to Apache Texera (Incubating), and we welcome new
contributors. Committer status recognizes sustained, high-quality contributions
to the project. New committers are nominated and elected by the PPMC based on:

- **Sustained contributions.** A track record of meaningful contributions to the
  project (code, documentation, reviews, releases, or community support) over a
  period of time, rather than a single burst of activity.
- **Quality.** Contributions that are well-designed, well-tested, and reviewed,
  along with a demonstrated ability to give constructive, careful reviews of
  other people's work.
- **Community involvement.** Active, respectful participation on the mailing
  lists and in issues and pull requests, and a willingness to help and mentor
  other contributors.
- **The Apache Way.** An understanding of how the Apache Software Foundation
  operates: community over code, lazy consensus, and open, asynchronous
  decision-making on the public mailing lists.

If you would like to get involved, see the [Community](/community/) page and the
project's [contributing guide](https://github.com/apache/texera/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
